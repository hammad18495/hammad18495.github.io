About me

Developer by craft, operator by background.

I bring something most developers at my stage don't: eight years on the front line of SaaS support and operations.

I'm Hammad Naeem Khan, a 6x certified Salesforce Developer at Arbisoft in Lahore, Pakistan. I build custom business logic and Lightning interfaces on the Salesforce Platform: Apex classes and triggers, asynchronous processing, Lightning Web Components and Flow automation, with rigorous test coverage and governor-limit discipline throughout.

Alongside development, I lead enterprise system integrations (Clever, ClassLink, OneRoster, SFTP) and design security models across profiles, roles, permission sets and sharing rules.

Before moving into development, I spent 8+ years leading support and operations teams, including supervising 60+ people and owning SLA and CSAT targets for clients such as Uber and edX. I've been the person who absorbs the fallout when an implementation ignores how people actually work.

"That shapes how I build: I design for the end user and the business process, not just the acceptance criteria."

Outside of client work I keep learning on Trailhead (Triple Star Ranger with 335 badges, 13 superbadges and 150,000+ points, and recognised as an Agentblazer Innovator '26). I use AI to learn faster, not to cut corners: practising with mock exams, understanding why an answer was wrong, and turning weak areas into strengths.
